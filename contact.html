What is this?

Someone you care about uses SecondLook, an app that notices when their texting looks very different from how they normally type — for example late on a night out — and gently checks in with them.

While they were sober, they chose you as their safe contact and agreed that if they don’t answer a check-in, you get a heads-up. That’s all this page is for.

  • You’ll only hear something if they didn’t respond to a check-in, or when they send a test.
  • Details are end-to-end encrypted: the notification only says they may need help; the rest unlocks on this page with a key that never leaves the link they sent you.
  • You can’t see their messages, and you can’t track them. Location is included only if they chose to share it.
  • If you think they’re in danger, call your local emergency number.
