Drinking breaks the very judgment you'd need to notice you're too drunk to drive. SecondLook lets you make the call while you're sober, then keeps that promise for you later tonight.

Free, and it runs in your browser. Nothing you type ever leaves your phone.

1 every 39 minutes

That's how often someone died in a drunk-driving crash in the US in 2022: 13,524 people, almost a third of all traffic deaths.

Source: NHTSA, Traffic Safety Facts 2022.

One app, two modes

You meet SecondLook in two very different states, so it looks after you differently in each.

Sober Mode is where you make the plan

Daytime, clear head, a little more detail.

  • Pick your safe contact and what they'll hear.
  • A 2-minute baseline of the sober you.
  • Tonight's plan in your own words.

Night Mode is where it keeps the promise

2am, one hand, a dark bar.

  • One big button at a time, where your thumb is.
  • Your own plan first: “You said you'd take an Uber.”
  • Large, calm text and a countdown that never shouts.

A promise, not surveillance

  1. You write the rules while sober.You choose your safe contact and what happens if you don't answer.

  2. Nothing happens behind your back.Every prompt, check-in and alert goes into a log you can read and download.

  3. Your sober decisions are protected.After something is flagged, settings lock for a few hours, so a drunk you can't switch them off.

  4. Your words stay yours.Only typing rhythm is checked, on your device. Messages are never stored or sent.

SecondLook can't measure blood alcohol, so it never tells you you're fine to drive.
