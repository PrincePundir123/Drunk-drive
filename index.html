For the nights your judgment isn’t on your side

The pause before a bad decision.

Being drunk breaks the very judgment you’d need to notice you’re too drunk to drive. SecondLook learns your sober self, notices when you drift from it, and helps you get home — following a plan you made while sober.

How it works

1

Learns your sober self

A 2-minute baseline: how fast you react, how steady your hand is, and how you normally type. It keeps learning quietly from the messages you send on normal days.

2

Notices the drift

Later in the night, it compares your typing rhythm, corrections, pauses and misspellings against your baseline — never against other people.

3

Steps in, gently

“This looks a little different from how you usually text — want a second look?” A small pause, not a lecture.

4

Brings in help

No answer, or signs get stronger? It checks in, offers a ride, and — only if you agreed while sober — lets your safe contact know.

Built on consent, not surveillance

  • You write the rules while sober. You pick your safe contact and decide what happens if you don’t respond.
  • Nothing happens behind your back. Every prompt, check-in and alert is written to a transparency log you can read and download.
  • Your sober decisions are protected. After something is flagged, safety settings lock for a few hours — an impaired you can’t quietly switch them off.
  • Private by design. Analysis runs on your device and only looks at timing and corrections. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Honest about limits. SecondLook can’t measure blood alcohol. It never tells you you’re “fine to drive”.
